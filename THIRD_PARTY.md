# Reuse and references

The solver, generated meshes, comparison scores, figures and text are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE). Installed dependencies retain their own licences. No NCAR source, archive, executable or reference-field archive, MPAS source or downloaded mesh, paper PDF or paper figure is included.

## NCAR STSWM 2.0

The README in the [Netlib distribution](https://www.netlib.org/chammp/) states: Copyright (C) 1992 University Corporation for Atmospheric Research, All Rights Reserved. The software is available for scientific research and educational purposes only and is not in the public domain. NCAR should be acknowledged as the software source in resulting research or publications. Substantial modifications should be identified as a derivative of the NCAR STSWM, and users are requested to send reprints. Read the full notices in the fetched archive before use.

[`fetch_reference.py`](scripts/fetch_reference.py) checks the archive against SHA-256 `385b001d0b116f86026e8df9f8c27f7749ed0533231c8b42541b1b8e2af2d9f1`. Fetched NCAR code remains outside this project's CC0 dedication.

The local build is a modified derivative of NCAR STSWM: it changes truncation and precision, replaces spectral netCDF output with grid-field output, and adds file-based initial conditions for the jet and diagnostic cases. The supplied build and patch files grant no additional rights over NCAR code. NCAR STSWM supplies the numerical reference fields used in the comparisons.

## Published methods and test cases

- Tomas Oppelstrup et al., [arXiv:2511.11542](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11542), supplies the reported parameters for the Domain Translation cost model. Its arXiv licence is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Only reported parameters are used; the paper, its code and its figures are not bundled.
- Thuburn et al. (2009), *Journal of Computational Physics* 228, 8321-8335, and Ringler et al. (2010), *Journal of Computational Physics* 229, 3065-3090, describe the TRSK mathematics implemented in [`trsk.py`](polarswe/trsk.py).
- Williamson et al. (1992), *Journal of Computational Physics* 102, 211-224, supplies the standard spherical shallow-water test cases.
- Galewsky et al. (2004), *Tellus A* 56, 429-440, supplies the unstable jet test.

The meshes are generated by this repository's code. Comparison errors are computed against analytic solutions or separately generated NCAR fields; they are not copied from published comparison tables.
